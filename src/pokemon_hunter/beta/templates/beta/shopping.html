Check “Include in group” on the cards, then capture their current quantities. The amount counts once and supplies no individual values. Changing membership or quantity requires a new reviewed estimate.

Amount and quantity formats

Use unsigned decimal amounts with a leading digit, no commas or exponent, at most six decimal places and a maximum of $1,000,000,000,000. Quantities are whole numbers from 1 to 100,000; the selected and known lot total cannot exceed 100,000. Only USD values are eligible. Blank costs stay unknown; enter zero only when confirmed. Final values round once to cents using half-even rounding.
