Photo batches

Reopening never analyzes or adds cards. Start a separate batch only for different physical cards.

1. Add photos

Try 12–15 card fronts on a contrasting surface, with visible gaps, all edges and sharp text. Keep cards parallel and lighting diffuse. Use fewer cards or a close-up for glare, overlap or unreadable text. Review the count yourself; a 16th card needs a retake.

JPEG, PNG or WebP, one frame. Maximum 8 MB and 24 MP per photo, five admissions including retakes, 40 MB and 75 candidate cards per batch. Uploading stages images locally without analysis. Unconfirmed images expire after seven days; edits do not extend this.

2. Review transmission

Selected card images and crops are sent through Codex CLI to OpenAI. Review them for personal information. Results can be wrong; you confirm additions. This uses your Codex account capacity. Stop cannot recall images already sent. Manual entry remains available.

Each explicit analysis sends one overview (at most 1600px / 2 MB), or the reviewed crops for that photo (at most 15, 1024px / 500 KB each, 7.5 MB combined). No original accompanies the crops. At most ten invocations per batch, two explicit attempts per stage, 75 active seconds per photo and invocation, 180 per batch. No automatic retries. Remaining subscription capacity and monetary cost: Unknown. Codex account policies apply; local cleanup does not establish service deletion or training policy.

Stop is independent of other controls. Consumed or uncertain attempts remain consumed.

3. Review crops and identities

Use detection proposals or add boxes manually. Correct each box and rotation, then confirm the layout/count before requesting identities. Editing a layout clears its identity choices. Model output establishes no ownership, authenticity, condition, grade or value. Missing catalog coverage and unreadable cards stay unresolved.

4. Review duplicates across photos

Compare the numbered thumbnails across all photos. Matching printings do not prove the same physical copy. For the same card, keep one and exclude the other. Keep both only for different physical copies. Not sure: exclude for now. A manually entered row can also represent a crop; exclude one of the pair.
