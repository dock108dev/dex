`POST /api/shopping/hunter/<run>/continue/` takes only the returned `cursor`.
It preserves plan/account/session/window/creation and rejects repeated, incompatible

`GET /api/shopping/hunter/?run_id=<run>&cursor=<result_cursor>` pages current local

current/minimum-next/manual bids remain scenarios with unknown final purchase price.

### Pokémon universe and coverage

The target is the Pokémon TCG across generations and eras, initially using the
existing US/English shopping rules. It includes owned cards and is independent of
Original 151/251, missing species and frozen goals. The owner clarified the
architecture: **listings first**. Query Pokémon singles across all years in
bounded batches, then match each offer to the existing local catalog and price
database. Do not send one search request per catalog card or restrict listing
retrieval to the current priced catalog. Batch lookup of existing admitted prices
is local; neither price fetching per offer nor a complete catalog import is needed
before the first useful hunt. Include supported Trainer/Energy singles when priced;
unmatched/unpriced offers stay explicit gaps.

Use a verified current marketplace singles category/game scope rather than assuming
the older supplied lot-category link identifies singles. The official
[Browse specification](https://developer.ebay.com/api-docs/master/buy/browse/openapi/3/buy_browse_v1_oas3.json)
supports up to 200 summaries per page and at most 10,000 retrievable results per
query. Thus 10,000 summaries at 200/page would take 50 Browse calls, not 10,000
per-card calls; this is arithmetic, not a market-size, quota or performance promise.
Larger result sets require bounded supported partitions or explicit truncation.

Record the local catalog/reference version and gaps to explain matching coverage.
Catalog expansion and bulk source refresh are independent improvements when needed,
not prerequisites to query the all-year offer universe. The current catalog,
artwork index and vintage price selector are different scopes; a larger artwork
index does not establish prices or exact seller identity.
See [catalog scope](catalogs.md#price-hunter-catalog-scope).

Report catalog, reference-eligible, planned, searched, returned, matched, comparable,
unpriced, skipped and truncated counts separately. Budget or page exhaustion is
partial coverage, not an empty market. Explain the searched scope and time window;
do not promise that every eBay listing was inspected. The official
[Browse search contract](https://developer.ebay.com/api-docs/buy/browse/resources/item_summary/methods/search)
and [call limits](https://developer.ebay.com/develop/api/buy/api_call_limits)
are planning inputs, not proof of this application's entitlement or remaining quota.
Partition/page only under a reviewed finite plan with durable consumed accounting;
do not silently extend SHOP-04's one-action limits or reuse closed qualification budgets.

### Matching and opportunity meaning

Join each offer to the same card, set/number, language, edition, finish/variant,
raw condition basis or literal grader/grade before calling its comparison applicable.
Plausible candidates may remain conditional; hard conflicts receive no confident
exact-card value. Reuse existing identity, source lifecycle and arithmetic services.
Keep loose-lot review available; a lot is never ranked as if its whole contents
were one matching single, and partial contents retain only a priced subtotal.

Show the live listing observation time and admitted reference dates separately.
An asking price is not an independent market-value source. Use an appropriately
dated, applicable market reference; stale, missing or withdrawn values cannot
become live merely because an offer was just fetched. The
[reference contract](price-snapshots.md#price-hunter-reference-requirements)
defines the remaining source work, including honest publisher-build dates.

For Buy It Now, distinguish listing amount, known shipping and complete/partial
delivered cost. For auctions, distinguish current bid, minimum next bid when
supplied, planned manual bid and unknown final price. Rank **below-reference
candidates**, not guaranteed bargains. Display dollar/percentage reference gaps
with their cost basis and uncertainties. A discount is not mathematical expected
value: real resale EV would additionally require defensible resale outcomes,
probabilities, fees, shipping, taxes, condition/authenticity risk and liquidity.
Unknown inputs stay unknown; the owner decides and places any bid/purchase.

### Added step: lot contents

The owner also selected loose-card lots as an added hunter step. Retrieve lot

and shared finite allowance. Parse the already available seller summary locally;

Automatically propose named cards, set/collector-number clues and quantities from

units, stated total card count and per-card quantities remain separate. A quantity

guess. Keep matched-card reference images and provenance/reasons available.

Sum applicable included-card references times their reviewed quantities, count the
whole-lot amount once and compare against known costs. Show dollar/percentage gap,
