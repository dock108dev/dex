# Find a lot that fills an original-151 gap

Updated for the lot-walkthrough fixes on 2026-11-07; installed verification is recorded separately. This guide covers finding,
reviewing and opening a seller listing. It does not describe a completed purchase.
Listings, prices, availability and temporary reviews change. Use your current collection.

## Choose a missing Pokémon

1. Open the existing Dex installation and sign in normally.
2. Open **Shopping → Search options → Find missing cards**.
3. Set **Card scope** to **Kanto · 1–151** and **Missing from** to
   **My current collection**. Use a frozen goal only when you intend its distinct rules.
4. Select a Pokémon in **Missing Pokémon**. This is a species gap, not a claim that
   every printing of a species you already own is collected. “Original 151” here means
   Kanto species, not the separate Scarlet & Violet—151 expansion.
5. Optionally choose **Prefer vintage**; the app says other qualifying eras remain
   eligible. Leave Set broad unless you specifically want one set.
6. Enter your all-in budget or baseline. For flexible shopping, choose **Baseline with
   opportunity exceptions**, select **Estimated collectible value** or **Net resale
   proceeds**, and write your exception rule. Higher-priced options remain visible;
   the app does not automatically qualify an exception or authorize a purchase.

## Run one bounded search

7. Click **Find lots** once. It searches up to two pages/40 summaries and automatically
   fetches details for at most two selected offers. Wait for the terminal result;
   do not resubmit merely because progress takes time.
8. If acquisition is unavailable because the usage policy expired, open **Lot search allowance → Usage settings** on the same page. Choose appropriate finite limits, timezone,
   nonrecurring window and expiry within your source permissions. Existing consumed
   operations still count. Saving settings does not itself search. Use **Refresh allowance** to check consumed operations without starting a search.
9. Read the progress and coverage counts. “Unprocessed” means full details were not
   assessed in this Hunt; it is not a zero-value lot. Buying-option and evidence filters
   only rearrange the acquired batch. A current bid can rise; unknown shipping is not free.

## Inspect a candidate

10. Choose **Assess lot**. Read the seller-supported contents, quantities and
    **Source evidence**. A title mention alone does not prove the missing card is included.
11. For a summary-only candidate, use **Assess this lot fully → Load full seller details** once. This uses provider capacity;
    opening the local review alone does not. Keep the original lease and expiry.
12. Open **View seller photos on eBay** or **Open seller listing**. Verify the exact
    offer, availability, item price, shipping, included cards, condition and return terms.
    Inspect the actual photo rather than relying on generated image descriptions.
13. Return to Dex and choose **Use offer in review**. Under **Correct identity and
    quantity**, select only a printing supported by the seller evidence you checked.
    Review language, set, collector number, edition, finish and physical quantity.
14. Including a proposed printing starts with one copy. Allocate other printings
    separately and read each entry’s allocated and remaining counts. All cards sharing
    a species name are not necessarily the same printing.
15. For itemized lots, select entries with the same supported traits, enter those traits
    under **Confirm shared traits across selected entries**, preview the exact cards and
    quantities, then confirm. Conflicting and ambiguous entries still need individual
    review. **Find a different printing** opens local search; quantity edits autosave.
    Keep unidentified cards explicit rather than assigning them an unsupported value.

## Make a purchase decision

16. Expand **Bid, budget and purchase costs**. Enter the applicable budget and known
    costs. Keep unknown tax/charges blank; enter zero only for an observed known zero.
    A planned auction bid is distinct from the current bid and a Buy It Now price.
17. Choose **Compare reviewed lot**. It reads eligible local references; it does not
    fetch fresh prices. Read source date, condition coverage, priced quantity and gaps.
18. Separate whole-lot value, an identified-card subtotal, and a publisher guide.
    An unspecified-condition publisher guide cannot establish resale proceeds,
    savings, a bid ceiling or a profit margin for a worn physical card. Remaining
    unidentified cards are unknown, not worthless. Higher-price budget exceptions
    require the evidence and costs specified by your policy.
19. Open the exact seller listing for the final decision. Recheck the current offer and
    final checkout total before buying. Dex does not buy or bid. Do not add the cards
    to owned inventory merely because a listing was reviewed.
20. Set **My lot decision** and record reasons, unknowns and next checks. **My temporary
    lot shortlist and decisions** retains up to 20 reviewed search results within their
    original evidence lifetime. Back shows the separate review status without changing
    the Hunt’s original processing counts. Reopening does not renew expiry.

## Record the decision

For each shortlist item, record: exact seller URL, observation time, missing species,
supported physical card identity and quantity, seller condition claim, asking/bid price,
shipping, unknown costs, reference source/date/condition and coverage, your decision,
and any step you had to handle outside the app. Keep personal collection details and
restricted source data private; use generalized examples in public documentation.

The initial walkthrough produced a candidate containing a photographed vintage Lapras.
The installed Moltres rerun then restored a three-card physical allocation, flexible
baseline, authored shipping and decision notes through the temporary journal. A
higher-priced offer remained visible but did not qualify as an exception. Both Moltres
decisions stayed **Needs evidence** because applicable value and final costs were
unavailable. Neither walkthrough established a full-lot value, resale margin,
final tax-inclusive total or a completed purchase. The corresponding
[feature backlog](lot-walkthrough-backlog-20261107.md) records the product gaps.

## Capture a useful walkthrough

Capture the actual application at the missing-species selection, budget policy,
allowance settings, allocation and decision steps. Embed each screenshot immediately
under its numbered step with a caption explaining what it proves. Record the actual
runtime and observation time privately. Never substitute a test account for the
customer app. Capture controls and authored decisions without retaining removable
source values, restricted seller evidence or credentials. A screenshot does not make a dated
listing or reference current. Keep private collection screenshots out of public Git.
