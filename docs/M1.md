## Behavior and preservation

Explicit collection goals use `collection-selected-species-v1` over selected canonical #001–251 targets; the earlier E2d `original-151-collection-v1` remains supported. Ownership and selected source are frozen independently from reviewed printing coverage. A source update changes the current collection after review; updating a goal creates a reviewed successor and retains its predecessor. Selecting an existing source uses an explicit confirmed journal selection without duplicating that source. Undo changes the current selection through journal state, and rejects removing a source required by a retained goal. Physical card criteria continue using catalog/exact-copy policy: a declared species never satisfies an unresolved exact printing.

Independent Pack lookup accepts any in-scope species, printing, selected targets, custom goal or missing scope, including owned species and Johto. Goal filters carry their frozen policy into lookup. Species above #251 remain in underlying metadata but are rejected as lookup targets. Retained catalog review, exact sealed bridges and confirmed physical booster membership determine expansion matches. Target gaps are explicit; an indexed printing does not establish its booster distribution. Save recomputes and validates the submitted scope/version server-side, then freezes ownership, filters, references and observations. Reopening after restart does not recalculate against newer ownership. Old reviewed-copy research remains intact.

Comparison shows supported target coverage and documented quantities, exact product identity, seller/source observations, dates, stock, shipping and freshness limitations. Current buying recommendations require existing purchase eligibility; this delivery establishes no eligible live offer. Unknown official contents, seller status or shipping remain unknown. No expected pulls, completion cost or unsupported best-value claim is introduced. eBay preserves search, comparison, reveal and private reopening with visible Sandbox/sample labels. Browser evidence uses samples and does not claim a live Sandbox listing or seller-review success.

Imports validate source bytes/hash, membership, policy, provenance, lineage, account ownership and remapped IDs; altered sources or memberships block the whole operation. Existing transaction and operation journal semantics provide idempotent confirmation and rollback. Collection JSON exports include referenced source bytes and frozen goals; saved Pack/eBay snapshots remain in the private database and its backup, rather than being portable collection JSON records.
